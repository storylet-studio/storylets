Field declarations take the family's one type list, shared with Patter's game data: `FieldType` is `text`, `multiline`, `number`, `boolean`, `enum`, `flags` or `quality`, with `string` read as a synonym of `text`. `FieldDecl` gains `stages` for a quality field, and `FIELD_TYPES` and `isTextFieldType` are exported.
