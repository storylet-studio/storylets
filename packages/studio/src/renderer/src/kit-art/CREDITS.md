# Kit pictures

The pictures on the kit gallery's tiles and on the welcome screen. Every one is a
public-domain or CC0 print from Wikimedia Commons, converted for the gallery: made
grayscale, the paper taken out so only the ink is left (as transparency), and cropped
or fitted to 5:3. The app draws the ink in its own colour, on a paper-toned plate in
the dark themes (the kit gallery brief, `storylet-studio/design/kit-gallery.md`, 8a).
